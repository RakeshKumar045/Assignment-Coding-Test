Contacting Support

Support is available by email around the clock, with a first response inside one business
day for standard plans and inside four hours for enterprise plans. Live chat is available
on weekdays from 9am to 6pm in the customer's local timezone.

Our refund policy allows a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the customer has not
exceeded 100 API calls. After the 14 day window, refunds are issued as account credit
instead of cash, and that credit can be applied to any future invoice.

Bug reports should include the workspace ID, a timestamp, and steps to reproduce. Reports
missing a timestamp are frequently unable to be triaged and are sent back to the reporter.
